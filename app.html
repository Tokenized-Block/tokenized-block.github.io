What “life” actually is

Life =price × supply, both re-readable on chain. Nobody paints it.
It goes downIf the price falls, life falls with it. A big block can become a small one overnight. Nobody refunds that.
⚠️ It is buyableMeasured 2026-09-09: 0.01 ETH moved a displayed market cap by 2 346 ETH — a lever of ×234 645, because supply is enormous. A big number here is not a big pile of money.
UnitMeasured in ETH, not dollars. We name the currency instead of mixing two.
No market yetA faded, smaller block has never been traded. That is not the same as being worthless.

Wallet

Balances, send, swap, deposit and withdraw.

Being built — in this order

  • Balances — reads only, no key needed.
  • Send — the transfer path exists and is tested; this needs the screen.
  • Swap — routing and calldata already measured against the deployed router.
  • Deposit / withdraw — see the question below.

⛔ A wallet per block holding funds, driven by a trading agent, is custody — a key that is not the user's, holding their money. This app is sold on “no key here, your wallet signs”, so that is the first promise we would give away, and it needs a licence in most places.

Same product, no custody: the agent proposes, the user's wallet signs. Identical on screen, nothing held, nothing to guard. That is the one I will build.

Pair your block

Back a block with a stock or another token at creation — what OpenLaunch does with tokenized equities.

Measured on their chain, not read off their page

  • The pairing is the quote currency — measured 2026-09-09 on three of their launches: the fee is chosen at launch (0 %, 1 %, 3 %), tickSpacing 200, no hooks, and the pair is whatever currency the pool is opened against — ETH, GITLAWB, COINc, AAPLc.
  • So pairing against a stock is the same gesture as pairing against ETH, with a different currency address. The mechanism we already have covers it.
  • What is missing — a registry of which tokenized-stock addresses are real. Inventing a ticker here would put someone's money into a token nobody vetted.
