Wallet

Balances, swap, deposit and withdraw — the exchange-style tab.

Not built yet — and here is exactly what is missing

  • Balances — reads only, no key. Straightforward, next.
  • Swap — the routing and calldata already exist and are tested; this tab needs the screen, not the engine.
  • Deposit / withdraw — depends on the question below.

⛔ A wallet per block, holding funds, driven by a trading agent, is custody. That means a key that is not the user's, holding the user's money. This app is sold on “no key here — your wallet signs”, so that would be the first promise given away, and it needs a licence in most places.

The same product exists without custody: the agent proposes, the user's wallet signs. Identical on screen, no key held, no deposits to guard. That is the one I will build — tell me if you meant something else.
