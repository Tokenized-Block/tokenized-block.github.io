What “life” actually is

Life =price × supply, both re-readable on chain. Nobody paints it.
It goes downIf the price falls, life falls with it. A big block can become a small one overnight. Nobody refunds that.
⚠️ It is buyableMeasured 2026-09-09: 0.01 ETH moved a displayed market cap by 2 346 ETH — a lever of ×234 645, because supply is enormous. A big number here is not a big pile of money.
UnitMeasured in ETH, not dollars. We name the currency instead of mixing two.
No market yetA faded, smaller block has never been traded. That is not the same as being worthless.
